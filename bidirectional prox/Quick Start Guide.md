# Quick Start Guide - Basic Welding Instructions

## Spool Loading - 2 lb Wire Spool

### Step-by-Step Loading

1. **Remove protective foam and cardboard** from welder before setup
2. **Open wire spool compartment** on front panel
3. **Insert 2 lb wire spool** onto spindle
4. **Secure with adapter and knob** (required for 10-12 lb spools)
5. **Feed wire through guides:**
   - Pass through brake pad
   - Through wire guides
   - Into feed mechanism
6. **Adjust feed tensioner** appropriately
7. **Thread through liner** to torch
8. **Install contact tip** in torch
9. **Test feed** before welding

### Important Notes
- **2 lb spool:** Direct loading (no adapter needed)
- **10-12 lb spool:** Requires adapter and knob
- Always remove packaging before use
- Check for tangles or kinks in wire

## Basic Welding Instructions

### Safety First

**WARNING - Serious Injury Risk!**

1. **Read all manual warnings** before use
2. **Read all instructions** before use
3. **Keep torch 2" clear** of grounded objects while plugged in
4. **Block area** around work zone
5. **Keep welder clear** of flammable materials
6. **Wear proper PPE:** helmet, gloves, protective clothing

### Contact Injury Prevention

- **Keep torch and wire clear** of grounded objects
- **While welder is plugged in** - maintain 2" clearance
- **Block work area** to prevent accidental contact
- **Unplug when not in use** or during setup

### Fire Prevention

- **Keep torch 2" from grounded objects** (minimum)
- **3" clearance** recommended for safety margin
- **Block work area** during operation
- **Remove flammable materials** from vicinity
- **Have fire extinguisher** readily available

## Spool Loading Details

### Wire Spool Components

```

               |
               v
    +------------------+
    |    Wire Spool    | <-- 2 lb capacity
    +------------------+
               |
               v
        Wire Spool Arm
               |
               v
         Feed Tensioner
               |
               v
          Wire Inlet
               |
               v
            Liner
               |
               v
         Roller Knob
               |
               v
    Wire Feed Mechanism
```

### Loading Procedure

1. **Loosen feed spool knob** to allow spool rotation
2. **Place 2 lb spool** on wire spool arm
3. **Secure with feed spool knob** (finger tight)
4. **Guide wire** through feed tensioner
5. **Thread through wire inlet**
6. **Pass through liner** (inside torch cable)
7. **Adjust roller knob** for proper tension
8. **Feed wire** through torch to contact tip
9. **Install correct size contact tip**
10. **Test wire feed** (without power if possible)

### Tension Adjustment

- **Too loose:** Wire slips, inconsistent feed
- **Too tight:** Crushes wire, causes bird-nesting
- **Just right:** Smooth, consistent feed
- **Test:** Squeeze wire - should feed but not crush

## Basic Welding Setup

### Pre-Welding Checklist

- [ ] Remove all protective packaging
- [ ] Load wire spool (2 lb or 10-12 lb with adapter)
- [ ] Thread wire through feed system
- [ ] Install correct contact tip
- [ ] Connect gas (if MIG welding)
- [ ] Set gas flow (15-20 CFH typical)
- [ ] Connect ground clamp
- [ ] Plug in welder
- [ ] Turn on main switch
- [ ] Set welding parameters
- [ ] Put on PPE (helmet, gloves, etc.)
- [ ] Clear work area (2" minimum clearance)
- [ ] Block work zone

### Parameter Settings (Starting Points)

#### For 18-22 Ga Material (0.040-0.030")
- **Wire:** 0.025" or 0.030"
- **Amperage:** 60-80 A
- **Voltage:** 14-17 V
- **Wire Speed:** 150-220 IPM
- **Gas Flow:** 15-20 CFH

#### For 16-18 Ga Material (0.060-0.040")
- **Wire:** 0.030"
- **Amperage:** 80-100 A
- **Voltage:** 17-20 V
- **Wire Speed:** 220-350 IPM
- **Gas Flow:** 15-20 CFH

## Welding Tips (From Manual)

### Technique Basics

1. **Maintain proper stick-out:** 3/8" to 1/2" (10-12mm)
2. **Hold torch at 10-15° angle** to work
3. **Push or pull technique:**
   - **Push:** Gun angled away from weld (better visibility)
   - **Pull:** Gun angled toward weld (deeper penetration)
4. **Travel speed:** Consistent, not too fast or slow
5. **Watch the puddle:** Maintain even width

### Common Problems & Solutions

| Problem | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Burn-through** | Too hot, too slow | Reduce amperage, increase speed |
| **Poor penetration** | Too cold, too fast | Increase amperage, slow down |
| **Undercut** | Too hot, wrong angle | Reduce heat, adjust angle |
| **Porosity** | Contamination, gas | Clean metal, check gas flow |
| **Bird-nesting** | Tension wrong, liner bad | Adjust tensioner, replace liner |
| **Wire burn-back** | Stick-out too short | Increase stick-out distance |

### Practice Recommendations

1. **Start on scrap material** of same thickness
2. **Practice straight beads** before joints
3. **Try lap joints** before butt joints
4. **Practice both push and pull** techniques
5. **Adjust settings** based on results
6. **Clean between welds** to see progress

## Maintenance Reminders

- [ ] Verify tensioner adjustment
- [ ] Clean contact tips

## Quick Reference

### Key Measurements
- **Minimum clearance:** 2" from grounded objects
- **Recommended clearance:** 3" for safety
- **Wire stick-out:** 3/8" - 1/2"
- **Torch angle:** 10-15°
- **2 lb spool:** Direct load
- **10-12 lb spool:** Requires adapter

### Safety Distances
- **Torch to grounded object:** 2" minimum
- **Work area clearance:** Block entire area
- **Welder to flammables:** As far as practical
- **Person to arc:** Protected by barrier

### When to Stop
- **Welder feels hot:** Allow cooling
- **Overheat light on:** Stop immediately
- **Unusual smells/sounds:** Power off and check
- **Poor weld quality:** Stop and adjust
