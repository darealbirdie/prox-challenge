# Safety Warnings & Symbols

## Safety Alert Symbol
This symbol is used to alert you to potential personal injury hazards. Obey all Safety messages that follow this symbol to avoid possible injury or death.

## Warning Levels

### ⚠️ DANGER - Death or Serious Injury
Indicates a hazardous situation which, **if not avoided, will result in death or serious injury.**

### ⚠️ WARNING - Death or Serious Injury  
Indicates a hazardous situation which, **if not avoided, could result in death or serious injury.**

### ⚠️ CAUTION - Minor or Moderate Injury
Indicates a hazardous situation which, **if not avoided, could result in minor or moderate injury.**

### ℹ️ NOTICE - Property Damage
Addresses practices not related to personal injury (equipment or property damage).

## General Safety Rules

### Read All Documentation
- Read manufacturer's instructions
- Review Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS)
- Do not use welder while tired or under influence
- Keep head out of fumes
- Use proper ventilation

### Before Use
1. Read and understand manufacturer's instructions
2. Review all safety warnings in this manual
3. Ensure proper grounding
4. Check for damaged cords or plugs
5. Verify adequate ventilation

### During Operation
1. Stay alert, watch what you are doing
2. Keep head out of fumes
3. Use enough ventilation or exhaust at arc
4. Do not breathe exhaust fumes
5. Keep torch and wire clear of grounded objects
6. Do not touch energized parts

### Fire Prevention
- Keep torch and wire clear of grounded objects while plugged in
- Remove flammable materials from work area
- Have fire extinguisher available
- Do not weld near combustibles

---

## Electrical Safety

### Electric Shock Prevention
**Electric shock can kill.**

1. Turn off, disconnect power, and discharge electrode to ground before:
   - Setting down torch/electrode holder
   - Before service
2. Do not touch energized parts
3. Do not use welder if switch does not turn on/off properly
4. Check grounding before use
5. Do not use if power cord or plug is damaged
6. Have damaged equipment repaired before use
7. Do not abuse the cord - never use for carrying, pulling, or unplugging
8. Keep cord away from heat, oil, sharp edges, or moving parts

### Grounding Requirements
- Check with qualified electrician if unsure about outlet grounding
- Do not use welder if power cord or plug is damaged
- Proper grounding prevents electric shock and death

---

## Fume & Gas Safety

### Inhalation Hazard
**Welding and plasma cutting produce toxic fumes.**

**Health Risks:**
- Exposure increases risk of developing certain cancers (larynx, lung)
- Can cause other respiratory diseases
- Metal fume fever possible

**Prevention:**
1. Keep head out of fumes
2. Do not breathe exhaust fumes
3. Use enough ventilation or exhaust at arc
4. Use approved respiratory protection when needed
5. Ensure adequate fresh air supply

---

## Hot Work Safety

### Burn Prevention
- Allow workpiece to cool before handling
- Wear appropriate protective clothing
- Use welding gloves
- Be aware of hot surfaces (welder, workpiece, slag)

### Eye & Face Protection
- Always use proper welding helmet with correct shade lens
- Protect bystanders with screens or barriers
- Never look at arc without protection
