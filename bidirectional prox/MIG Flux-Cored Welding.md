# MIG / Flux-Cored Wire Welding

## Setup Instructions

### Before Setup
1. Turn power switch OFF
2. Unplug welder from power source
3. Read entire safety section before setup or use

### To Prevent Serious Injury From Accidental Operation
- Turn power switch OFF and unplug welder before setup
- Remove protective foam and cardboard from welder

---

## MIG Process Specifications

### Power Input Options
- **120 VAC / 60 Hz** (Standard)
- **240 VAC / 60 Hz** (High Power)

### Current Input at Output
| Voltage | 120V Setting | 240V Setting |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Current** | 20.8 A at 100 A output | 25.5 A at 200 A output |

### Welding Current Range
- **120V:** 30 – 140 A
- **240V:** 30 – 220 A

### Rated Duty Cycles
| Voltage | Rating |
|---------|--------|
| **120V** | 40% @ 100 A<br>100% @ 75 A |
| **240V** | 25% @ 200 A<br>100% @ 115 A |

### Maximum OCV (Open Circuit Voltage)
- **86 VDC**

## Weldable Materials - MIG

### Mild Steel
- Standard welding capability
- Most common application

### Stainless Steel
- Requires proper gas mixture
- Use appropriate wire type

### Aluminum
- **Requires optional Spool Gun**
- Cannot use standard MIG setup
- Special feeding mechanism needed

---

## Wire Specifications - MIG

### Solid Core Wire Sizes
- 0.025" (0.6 mm)
- 0.030" (0.8 mm)
- 0.035" (0.9 mm)

### Wire Feed Speed
- Measured in IPM (Inches Per Minute)
- Adjusted based on:
  - Material thickness
  - Wire size
  - Welding current
  - Voltage setting

## MIG Gun Setup

### Components
- MIG Gun / Spool gun compartment
- Gas outlet (for MIG)
- Wire feed socket
- Positive socket
- Negative socket

### Spool Gun (Optional)
- Required for aluminum welding
- Sold separately
- Connect gas hose to Spool Gun Gas Outlet when using
- Use appropriate adapter and knob for 10-12 lb spool

### Wire Spool Loading
- 2 lb spool: Direct loading
- 10-12 lb spool: Requires adapter and knob

## MIG Process Tips

### Wire Feed Settings
- Match to wire diameter
- Adjust for material thickness
- Test on scrap material first
- Refer to selection chart for guidance

### Gas Flow
- Maintain proper coverage
- Check for leaks
- Adjust for wind conditions

### Gun Angle
- Maintain consistent angle
- Typically 10-15 degrees
- Push or pull technique as needed

---

## Duty Cycle Understanding

### What It Means
- **40% @ 100A:** Can weld 4 minutes out of 10 at 100A
- **100% @ 75A:** Can weld continuously at 75A

### Why It Matters
- Prevents overheating
- Allows cooling time
- Extends welder life

### 120V vs 240V
- 240V provides higher duty cycle at high amperage
- Better for thicker materials
- Requires 240V outlet

---

## Troubleshooting - MIG

### Poor Wire Feed
- Check tensioner adjustment
- Verify liner condition
- Inspect drive rolls
- Check for kinks in cable

### Porosity in Weld
- Check gas flow
- Verify gas mixture
- Clean base metal
- Check for wind

### Burn-Back
- Adjust wire feed speed
- Check contact tip size
- Maintain proper stick-out
