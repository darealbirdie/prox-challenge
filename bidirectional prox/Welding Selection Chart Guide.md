# Welding Selection Chart Guide

## How to Choose Your Welding Process

### Step 1: Identify Your Input Voltage

**IMPORTANT! Do you have 120 Volt or 240 Volt?**

- **120V:** Standard household outlet
  - Suitable for: Light to medium duty welding
  - Best for: Hobbyists, home repairs, thin materials
  - Available amperage: Lower (30-140A MIG, 10-125A TIG)

- **240V:** Heavy-duty outlet (dryer/stove type)
  - Suitable for: Production, thicker materials
  - Best for: Professional use, heavy fabrication
  - Available amperage: Higher (30-220A MIG, 10-175A TIG)

### Step 2: Determine Material Type

#### Material Categories

**MILD STEEL**
- Most common structural steel
- Easy to weld
- Applications: General fabrication, automotive, construction
- All processes work well

**STAINLESS STEEL**
- Corrosion resistant
- Requires proper technique
- Applications: Food service, marine, decorative
- Best processes: TIG (quality), MIG (with proper gas)

**ALUMINUM**
- Lightweight, conducts heat well
- Requires special equipment
- Applications: Automotive, marine, aircraft
- Best processes: TIG (AC), MIG (with Spool Gun)

**CASTINGS**
- Poured metal parts
- May have porosity
- Requires pre-heat
- Best process: TIG (precise heat control)

**MAGNESIUM ALLOYS**
- Lightweight, flammable
- Specialized welding
- Best process: AC TIG only

### Step 3: Determine Material Thickness

#### Thickness Ranges

**VERY THIN**
- 18 Gauge to 5/16" (1.0mm to 8mm)
- Includes: Sheet metal, body panels, thin tubing
- Best process: TIG (precise control)
- Alternative: MIG (with care, low settings)

**LIGHT**
- 22 Gauge to 3/8" (0.8mm to 9.5mm)
- Includes: Automotive panels, light fabrication
- Best process: Any (MIG easiest)
- All processes suitable

**MODERATE**
- 10 Gauge to 4" (3.2mm to 102mm)
- Includes: Structural steel, heavy fabrication
- Best process: MIG (production), Stick (versatile)
- Multiple passes often required

**THICK**
- 24 Gauge to 3/16" (0.6mm to 4.8mm)
- Note: Overlaps with light category
- Best process: Depends on material type

### Step 4: Consider Your Application

#### Application Categories

**SHOWREEL / DISPLAY**
- For: Demonstrations, teaching, samples
- Requirements: Clean, attractive welds
- Best process: TIG (cosmetic quality)

**STEEL FABRICATION**
- For: Structural, construction, general
- Requirements: Strength, efficiency
- Best process: MIG (speed), Stick (versatility)

**STEEL TUBING**
- For: Frames, roll cages, furniture
- Requirements: Precision, penetration
- Best process: TIG (control), MIG (speed)

**CHROME/MOLYBDENUM STEEL**
- For: High-strength, high-temp applications
- Requirements: Proper heat treatment
- Best process: TIG (precise control)

**SHEET METAL**
- For: Ductwork, enclosures, panels
- Requirements: No burn-through, clean welds
- Best process: TIG (best), MIG (with care)

**ALUMINUM FABRICATION**
- For: Marine, automotive, aircraft
- Requirements: Clean, strong welds
- Best process: TIG (traditional), MIG with Spool Gun

### OMNIPRO 220 Process Capabilities

#### MIG/Flux-Cored Welding
- **Best for:** Production, thick materials, easy operation
- **Material:** Mild steel, stainless (with proper gas)
- **Thickness range:** 18G to 3/8"+ (with skill)
- **Setup:** Easiest to learn
- **Speed:** Fastest deposition

#### TIG Welding
- **Best for:** Quality, thin materials, precision
- **Material:** All types (steel, stainless, aluminum)
- **Thickness range:** 26G to 1/4"+ (with skill)
- **Setup:** Most complex
- **Quality:** Highest, cosmetic welds

#### Stick Welding
- **Best for:** Versatility, outdoor, maintenance
- **Material:** Mild steel, stainless (with proper rods)
- **Thickness range:** 14G to 1/2"+ (multiple passes)
- **Setup:** Simple
- **Portability:** Best (no gas needed)

### Quick Selection Guide

**Choose MIG if:**
- You want easiest operation
- Production speed matters
- Working primarily mild steel
- Indoor, controlled environment
- Thicker materials (14G and up)

**Choose TIG if:**
- Quality and appearance critical
- Welding thin materials
- Working stainless or aluminum
- Precision control needed
- Willing to learn complex process

**Choose Stick if:**
- Working outdoors/windy conditions
- Need maximum portability
- Maintenance/repair work
- Dirty or rusty materials
- No gas supply available
