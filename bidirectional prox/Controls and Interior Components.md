# Controls & Interior Components

## Front Panel Controls

### Primary Controls

#### Control Knob
- **Function:** Adjusts welding parameters
- **Type:** Rotary control
- **Location:** Center of panel
- **Operation:** Turn to select settings

#### LCD Display
- **Type:** Digital readout
- **Location:** Left side of panel
- **Shows:** Current welding parameters
- **Readings:** Amperage, voltage, wire speed

#### Power Knob
- **Function:** Main power control
- **Location:** Right side of panel
- **Operation:** Turn to adjust power output

#### Main Switch
- **Function:** ON/OFF control
- **Type:** Toggle or rocker switch
- **Safety:** OFF when not in use
- **Storage:** Compartment available

---

## MIG Gun & Spool System

### MIG Gun Storage
- **Compartment:** Built-in storage on front panel
- **Purpose:** Protect gun when not in use
- **Note:** Keep clean and dry

### Spool Gun Socket
- **Purpose:** Connection for optional Spool Gun
- **Use Case:** Aluminum welding
- **Requirement:** Optional Spool Gun (sold separately)
- **Gas Connection:** Separate Spool Gun Gas Outlet

### Spool Gun Gas Outlet
- **Function:** Gas supply for Spool Gun
- **Connection:** Hose from gas regulator
- **Note:** Only use when Spool Gun is connected

---

## Wire Feed System

### Wire Feed Socket
- **Function:** Connection for wire feed mechanism
- **Location:** Rear panel
- **Connection:** To wire feed unit

### Wire Feed Mechanism Components

#### Cold Wire Feed Switch
- **Function:** Manual wire feed control
- **Use:** Feed wire without arc
- **Location:** Interior controls

#### Feed Tensioner
- **Function:** Adjusts wire feed tension
- **Adjustment:** Prevents bird-nesting
- **Location:** Wire feed mechanism
- **Note:** Proper tension critical for smooth feed

#### Wire Spool Arm
- **Function:** Holds wire spool
- **Capacity:** 2 lb spool standard
- **Adapter:** Required for 10-12 lb spool

#### Feed Spool Knob
- **Function:** Adjusts spool resistance
- **Purpose:** Maintains consistent feed
- **Location:** Wire spool mechanism

#### Roller Knob
- **Function:** Adjusts drive roller tension
- **Purpose:** Proper wire grip
- **Note:** Too tight = crushed wire; too loose = slippage

#### Wire Inlet
- **Function:** Entry point for wire
- **Location:** Wire feed mechanism
- **Note:** Keep clean and free of debris

#### Liner
- **Function:** Guides wire to torch
- **Material:** Plastic or Teflon
- **Maintenance:** Inspect for wear/damage
- **Replacement:** When kinked or worn

#### Foot Pedal Socket (Control)
- **Function:** Connection for foot pedal
- **Use:** Amperage control during weld
- **Optional:** Hand control alternative

---

## Electrical Connections

### Positive Socket
- **Function:** Work lead connection
- **Connection:** Ground clamp/cable
- **Note:** Secure connection required

### Negative Socket
- **Function:** Electrode lead connection
- **Connection:** Torch/gun lead
- **Note:** Proper polarity critical

### Power Cable Socket
- **Function:** AC power input
- **Voltage:** 120V or 240V
- **Note:** Match to outlet voltage

---

## Gas System (MIG/TIG)

### Gas Outlet
- **Function:** Shielding gas supply
- **Connection:** Regulator hose
- **Gas Type:** Argon/CO2 mix (MIG) or Pure Argon (TIG)
- **Flow Rate:** Adjustable via regulator

### Gas Flow Control
- **Typical Range:** 10-30 CFH
- **MIG Setting:** 15-20 CFH typical
- **TIG Setting:** 15-20 CFH typical
- **Adjustment:** On gas regulator

## Interior Access

### Maintenance Access
- **Panels:** Remove for cleaning
- **Components:** Inspect regularly
- **Cooling Fan:** Keep unobstructed
- **Wiring:** Check for damage

### Overheat Protection
- **Feature:** Automatic shutdown
- **Indicator:** Overheat shutdown light
- **Action:** Allow to cool completely
- **Restart:** After cooling period

## Quick Reference - Component Functions

### Wire Feed Path
```
Spool → Spool Arm → Feed Spool Knob → Wire Inlet → 
Liner → Roller Knob → Feed Tensioner → Cold Wire Switch → 
Wire Feed Socket → Torch → Contact Tip
```

### Electrical Path
```
Power Cable → Power Switch → Control Knob → 
Transformer → Output → Negative Socket (Torch)
                                      → Positive Socket (Work)
```

### Gas Path (MIG/TIG)
```
Gas Bottle → Regulator → Gas Outlet → Torch → Nozzle → Arc
```
