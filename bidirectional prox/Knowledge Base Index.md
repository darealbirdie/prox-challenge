## 📊 Technical Data Summary

### OMNIPRO 220 Capabilities

| Process | 120V Range | 240V Range | Best For |
|---------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **MIG** | 30-140A | 30-220A | Production, ease |
| **TIG** | 10-125A | 10-175A | Quality, precision |
| **Stick** | Wide range | Wide range | Versatility |

### Material Compatibility

| Material | MIG | TIG | Stick |
|----------|-----|-----|-------|
| Mild Steel | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Stainless | ✓ | ✓ | △ |
| Aluminum | △ (Spool Gun) | ✓ | ✗ |

### Duty Cycles (120V)

| Process | Rating | Continuous |
|---------|--------|------------|
| **MIG** | 40% @ 100A | 75A |
| **TIG** | 40% @ 125A | 90A |
