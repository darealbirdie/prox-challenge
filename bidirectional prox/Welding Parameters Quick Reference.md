## Material Specifications

### Mild Steel
- **Most Common:** A36, 1018, 1020
- **Weldability:** Excellent
- **Pre-heat:** Not required (under 1")
- **Wire Types:** ER70S-3, ER70S-6 (MIG)
- **Rods:** E7018, E6013 (Stick)

### Stainless Steel
- **Common Types:** 304, 316
- **Wire Types:** ER308L, ER309L (MIG)
- **Rods:** 308L, 309L (TIG)
- **Gas:** 100% Argon or Ar/He mix
- **Note:** Keep heat input low

### Aluminum
- **Common Types:** 6061, 5052
- **Wire:** 4043, 5356 (Spool Gun required)
- **Rods:** 4043, 5356 (TIG)
- **Tungsten:** Pure or Zirconiated (AC)
- **Gas:** Pure Argon or Ar/He mix
- **Note:** Requires AC for TIG

## Shielding Gas Selection

### MIG Mild Steel
- **Best:** 75% Argon / 25% CO2
- **Alternative:** 100% CO2 (more spatter)
- **Flow Rate:** 15-25 CFH

### MIG Stainless Steel
- **Best:** 98% Argon / 2% CO2
- **Alternative:** Tri-mix (90% He, 7.5% Ar, 2.5% CO2)
- **Flow Rate:** 15-20 CFH

### MIG Aluminum
- **Required:** 100% Argon
- **Thick Material:** 75% He / 25% Ar
- **Flow Rate:** 20-30 CFH

### TIG (All Materials)
- **Standard:** 100% Argon
- **Aluminum Thick:** 50-75% He / Balance Ar
- **Flow Rate:** 15-20 CFH

## Quick Selection Guide

### What Material?
- **Mild Steel:** MIG (easiest), Stick (versatile), TIG (quality)
- **Stainless:** TIG (best), MIG (with proper gas)
- **Aluminum:** TIG (thin), MIG with Spool Gun (thick)

### What Thickness?
- **Thin (<18 Ga):** TIG or MIG (low settings)
- **Light (18-14 Ga):** Any process
- **Medium (14-10 Ga):** MIG or TIG
- **Thick (>10 Ga):** MIG (240V) or Stick

### Production Level?
- **High:** MIG
- **Medium:** TIG or Stick
- **Low/Repair:** Stick

### Location?
- **Indoor:** Any process
- **Outdoor/Windy:** Stick
- **Confined:** TIG or Stick
